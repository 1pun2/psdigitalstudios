Treatments we offer

From a routine check-up to a complete smile makeover, you can get everything in one clinic.

  • Check-up and cleaning

    A thorough exam and professional clean to keep teeth and gums healthy.

  • Fillings

    Tooth-coloured fillings that repair cavities and blend in naturally.

  • Root canal treatment

    Relief from deep infection or pain, so you can keep your natural tooth.

  • Teeth whitening

    Safe, supervised whitening for a brighter smile before a big day.

  • Braces and aligners

    Straighten your teeth with options to suit your age, budget and lifestyle.

  • Dental implants

    A long-term way to replace missing teeth so you can eat and smile with confidence.

  • Children's dentistry

    Friendly visits that help children feel calm and build good habits early.

  • Emergency care

    Tooth pain, a broken tooth or swelling? We reserve time each day for urgent cases.

Common questions

How often should I get a check-up?

Most dentists suggest a check-up and cleaning about every six months. Your dentist may advise a different gap depending on your teeth and gums.

Do you treat children?

Yes. We welcome children and take time to make the visit calm and friendly. Bring your child in early so dental visits feel normal.

What should I do in a dental emergency?

Call or WhatsApp us straight away and tell us what happened. We keep time free for urgent cases and will book you in as early as we can.

Will I know the cost before treatment?

Yes. Your dentist will explain your options and the cost before starting, so there are no surprises.

How do I book an appointment?

Use the form at the top of this page or message us on WhatsApp. We will reply to confirm your time.
