How a project runs

Four stages, one point of contact throughout

You deal with one project lead from the first site visit to the final styling day, not a rotating cast of contractors.

01

Consultation

We visit the space, listen to how you live or work, and understand the brief before drawing a single line.

02

Concept & Design

Layout, materials and a full design direction, presented as drawings and a real, physical materials board.

03

Execution

We manage the build and every vendor, so you have one point of contact from demolition through to install.

04

Reveal

A finished space, styled and photographed, handed back to you ready to live in from day one.
